Privacy Policy

Car Racing Multiplayer 3D (Android, package com.carracing.multiplayer3d) · Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Car Racing Multiplayer 3D (“the game”, “we”) handles, why, and the choices you have. In short:

  • Your race progress and settings are saved on your own device.
  • The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects some information from your device.
  • Where sign-in is available, you can play with a guest account or sign in with Google through Google Firebase.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: race results, medals, coins, unlocked and owned cars, selected car, car tuning and your chosen driver name.
  • Your settings: graphics quality, sound volume, steering and control layout choices.
  • Ghost lap recordings used for time trials.
  • Simple counters used to limit how often ads are shown.

We do not upload this game data to our own servers. It is deleted when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the game. Your device’s own backup features may copy app data according to your device settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads, full-screen ads between races and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for extra in-game credits. These are provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix problems, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device or app identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • how you interact with the app and with ads;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash data, device model and operating system version.

Depending on your settings and where you live, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Google processes this information under its own policies: see Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your advertising choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before ads use your information.
  • On Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation, in your device settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Accounts and sign-in (Google Firebase)

Some versions of the game offer accounts through Firebase Authentication, a Google service. In versions without this feature you play as a local guest and no account information leaves your device.

  • Guest account: Firebase creates a random user ID for your installation. We do not ask for your name or email address.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose it, we receive your Google account’s name, email address, user ID and, if your account provides one, a profile picture link. We use these only to create and recognise your account and to show who is signed in.

Firebase also processes technical information such as your IP address and device information to keep sign-in secure. See Privacy and Security in Firebase. You can sign out at any time on the Profile screen.

Deleting your data and account

  • Data on your device: clear the app’s data in Android settings, or uninstall the game.
  • Your account: signing out does not delete it. To ask us to delete your account and the information linked to it, contact us as described below. We will delete it within 30 days of confirming that the request comes from you. Information we are legally required to keep may be retained.
  • Advertising information: it is held by Google; use the choices above or Google’s privacy tools.

Device sensors

If you choose tilt steering, the game reads the phone’s motion sensor while you play. The readings are used immediately to steer your car and are neither stored nor sent anywhere.

Permissions

The game uses internet and network-state access, and the advertising ID, for ads and sign-in. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos or files.

Sharing of information

Information described above is processed by Google as the provider of the advertising and sign-in services. Apart from that, we do not share your information unless the law requires it. Google may process information in countries other than your own. Google Play, from which you may install the game, handles information under its own privacy policy.

How long information is kept and how it is protected

Account information is kept until the account is deleted. Information held by Google for advertising is kept according to Google’s policies. Data sent to Google’s services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age that applies in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Online multiplayer

The current version has no online multiplayer. If online play or other new features that handle information are added, this policy will be updated before they are released.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.

Contact

For questions or requests about this policy, open an issue at github.com/chaubeyprashant/car-racing-multiplayer-3d-privacy/issues. Issues are public: do not post your email address or other personal information there. Say what you need, and we will arrange a private way to continue.
