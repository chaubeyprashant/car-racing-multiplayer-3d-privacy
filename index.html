Privacy Policy

Car Racing Multiplayer 3D (Android) · Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Car Racing Multiplayer 3D (“the game”, “we”) handles. In short: the game does not collect your personal information and does not send it to us or to anyone else.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services from third parties.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following only on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • Your progress: race results, medals, coins, unlocked and owned cars, selected car and car tuning.
  • Your settings: graphics quality, sound volume, steering and control layout choices.
  • Ghost lap recordings used for time trials.

This data is not transmitted from your device. It is deleted when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the game.

Device sensors

If you choose tilt steering, the game reads the phone’s motion sensor (accelerometer/gyroscope) while you play. The readings are used immediately to steer your car and are neither stored nor sent anywhere.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Third-party services

The current version of the game does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics or social services. Google Play, the platform from which you may install the game, processes information under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Online features

The game’s name refers to multiplayer racing. If online features are added in the future, this policy will be updated before they are released to explain what is collected and why.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/chaubeyprashant/car-racing-multiplayer-3d-privacy/issues.
